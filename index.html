Familiar photos.

Bring together memories from your albums and folders. Choose how photos change and how information appears, then enjoy a frame that fits your space.

A frame filled with your photos

Your sources, together

Enjoy Apple Photos albums on supported devices, selected folders, and SMB or WebDAV photos on your NAS. Access to each source depends on your device and server.

Made for your space

Choose transition timing, effects, and photo fitting. Display a clock, folder or album names, and available photo dates in a separate area at the top or bottom.

Controls that fit your device

Use touch on iPhone and iPad, windows and a keyboard on Mac, or the Apple TV remote. App lock uses the authentication options available on your device.

Remove ads with a one-time purchase, without a subscription, and restore an existing purchase. Check the price for your region in Apple's purchase interface before paying.

Mac · Apple TV

MyFrame

A separate paid download without ads, currently being prepared for release. It does not include the Google advertising SDK.

This is a separate purchase from ad removal in MyFrame lite. Buying ad removal in Lite does not include the paid download of MyFrame.

Connecting your photos

Network photos require a reachable server address and permission to read the files. Cloud folders are available within the capabilities of the operating system's file picker and your installed file providers. SFTP uses an optional bridge running on a Mac. Direct Google Photos connections are not currently available.

The apps read selected photos and create viewing caches. They do not modify or delete external originals, or use photos, album names, or NAS account information in advertising requests. For information about the advertising SDK's separate data handling, see advertising and privacy.
